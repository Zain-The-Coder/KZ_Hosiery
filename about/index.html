ABOUT US

Established in Pakistan in February 1987.

K.Z Hosiery was founded with the vision of providing high-quality knitted garments featuring appliqués and embroidery. Under the leadership of Managing Director Mr. Tanveer Ahmed, who brings extensive experience in the field, we have a deep understanding of the needs and expectations of markets in France, the UK, the USA and Spain, as well as the specific nuances of their local businesses. Our production facility has a capacity of 100,000+ pieces per month, covering T-shirts, rompers, shorts, leggings, hooded pullovers and zip-ups, pajamas, polo shirts and Henleys.
