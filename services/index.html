OUR SERVICES

Support across the full production cycle.

01

Product development

Our expert merchandisers work closely with the product development team to create high-quality textile products. From prototypes to pre-production samples, we ensure every detail meets and exceeds buyer expectations.

02

Production monitoring

We oversee production at every stage, using control charts and frequent factory visits to ensure each product meets the highest standards and is delivered on time.

03

Quality control

Our quality assurance team conducts rigorous inline and final inspections, ensuring every textile product meets international quality standards and customer benchmarks.

04

Sourcing

We offer tailored sourcing solutions based on each customer's requirements, leveraging our extensive supplier network. Continuous supplier evaluations ensure buyers receive the best in quality, pricing and delivery.

05

Logistics support

Our experienced shipping department manages space booking, dispatching and real-time shipment monitoring, ensuring seamless communication with carriers until goods reach their final destinations.

06

Communication

We provide daily updates on production and shipping, with fully computerized offices ensuring smooth communication with our customers.
