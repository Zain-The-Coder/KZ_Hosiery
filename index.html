WHO WE ARE ??

Nearly four decades of knitwear manufacturing in Pakistan..

K.Z Hosiery was established in February 1987 with the vision of providing high-quality knitted garments featuring appliqués and embroidery. Under Managing Director Mr. Tanveer Ahmed, the team understands the expectations of each export market and the nuances of its local business.

Our multi-generational artisans work shoulder-to-shoulder with modern automated production engineers, guaranteeing that every seam, ribbed cuff, and brushed fleece surface stands ready for premium European and North American retail shelves.
